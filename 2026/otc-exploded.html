Binary Star System
Olivia Tremor Control has two primary songwriters — Hart and Doss — each with their own gravitational pull. The band node floats between them. Expanding either songwriter reorients the map around their individual world. This is a new node type: the binary star.
②
Expansion
The core interaction. Selecting any node pulls it to center, reorienting the entire map around it. Pull Hart to center — Circulatory System moves to orbit 1. Pull Elephant 6 — the full collective comes into focus. Every expansion is a new rabbit hole.
③
Orbit Depth & Information Density
Inner orbits display rich contextual data — releases, dates, collaborators, liner notes. Outer orbits show only enough to invite curiosity. The Kinks and Beatles sit at orbit 3 not because they're less important — because the *why* of their connection is what New Worlds surfaces on expansion.
④
Node Taxonomy
Shape communicates type at a glance. Artists and bands are circles. Labels and collectives are squares. Places and origin points are triangles. The Elephant 6 collective is a square — an institutional node, not a person. Ruston, Louisiana is a triangle — the origin that made everything possible.
⑤
Edge Type Encodes the Why
Solid edges indicate direct relationships. Dashed edges are influence, sonic parallel, or thematic connection. GBV connects via 60s pop melodic ambition — not lo-fi methodology. Sebadoh connects via lo-fi innovation. Syd Barrett via psychedelic rupture. Same surface quality, three different edges.
⑥
Methodology as First-Class Node
4-Track Home Recording is not an artist or a label — it's a way of working. Making it a node surfaces an entire lineage: OTC → GBV → Sebadoh → R. Stevie Moore. No algorithm creates this connection. It requires understanding that fidelity is sometimes the point, and sometimes completely beside it.
