①
EXPANSION
The core interaction. Selecting any node pulls it to center, reorienting the entire map around it. The previous center becomes a first-orbit node. This is how you follow the rabbit hole.
②
ORBIT DEPTH & INFORMATION DENSITY
Inner orbits display rich contextual data — releases, dates, collaborators, liner notes. Outer orbits show only enough to invite curiosity. Detail rewards proximity.
③
NODE TAXONOMY
Shape communicates type at a glance. Artists and bands are circles. Labels are squares. Studios and venues are triangles. Each type carries different contextual data.
④
EDGE WEIGHT
Solid edges indicate direct relationships — shared recordings, co-releases, formal collaboration. Dashed edges are indirect — shared influence, geographic proximity, label overlap.
⑤
HISTORY TRAIL
Every expansion is recorded as a breadcrumb. The trail shows where you've been — and makes the rabbit hole navigable. You can always step back.
⑥
MANUAL CURATION
The graph is hand-built, not algorithmically generated. Every edge is a considered decision. This is the argument against the black box — human context, made visible.
