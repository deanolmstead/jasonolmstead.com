Section
02
Overview

As Director of Design at Role, I worked closely with the startup's co-founders to develop the virtual tabletop's product strategy and design aesthetic. Designed to provide flexibility and customization, the tabletop gives players robust tools to configure game rooms to any style of play.

Following the release of the virtual tabletop, I led the development of a unified design system for the dashboard, marketplace, and mobile web app. I am the sole design contributor to work shown except where noted.

Competitive Analysis

Overly complex and requiring external tools

With embedded video/audio and an innovative RPG game builder, Role stands out in the virtual tabletop space with a self-contained, easy-to-use interface.

Mapping out relationships and surfacing core features

Since each feature has a wide range of options, it took several passes to organize flows that prioritize most-used features while identifying and unifying secondary actions.
